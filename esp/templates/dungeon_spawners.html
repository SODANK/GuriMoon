How spawners work

Each spawner keeps target sites of one dungeon alive in systems matching its filters, up to max / system in any one system. Sites appear on the overview. With Beacon on overview unticked they are hidden cosmic signatures, found only with scanner probes. This applies to new sites only. Sites go in orbit of a planet or in deadspace, away from celestials. A site despawns after every NPC and released wave is cleared. A site nobody clears stays. No regions picked means every region except Jove and rogue-drone space. Pick a drone region to spawn there.
