How the numbers work

Seize sec and atk sec are minimum system securities: customs.go acts when the system's security is at or above the value, and treats 0 or a negative as off. Officers only exist at {{printf "%.2f" .CustomsMinSec}} and above, so anything at or below that figure applies in all of this faction's policed space, and anything above {{printf "%.1f" .MaxSystemSec}} never applies anywhere.

Fine × multiplies the item's basePrice: the pilot pays quantity × basePrice × this, with no security gate, so any scan that finds the item charges it. An item with no basePrice yields no fine at all.

Loss is the faction standing penalty from the CCP data. customs.go does not apply it yet — confiscation, fines and attack are wired, standing loss is not, so the column is stored and displayed but inert.
