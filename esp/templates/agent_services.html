Agent Services

The non-mission services an agent offers: locating pilots, repairing negative security status, and the gifts/rumours stubs. Distribution is by division — a ticked cell grants the service to every agent of that division at or above the minimum level — with per-agent overrides on top. The info window's Agent Info tab and the conversation choices are built entirely from this page's data; the dialogue table below is every line the player can read, with the choices that lead in and out of each.
