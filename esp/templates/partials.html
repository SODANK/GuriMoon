Unarmed: the NPC never shoots. In belts an unarmed, moving NPC runs away when attacked. It can still use electronic warfare.

Sensors & signature

How hard the NPC is to jam and how easy it is to hit.

Racial ECM of the matching type jams it best.
Higher resists ECM. Frigates around 8, cruisers 14, battleships 20.
How big a target it is: bigger is locked faster and hit harder. Blank uses 125 m.
Sensor strength 0: every ECM cycle against this NPC jams it.

Behaviour

How the NPC moves around its target and when it engages. Stance (aggressive, defensive, passive) is set where it spawns.

Distance it orbits its target at.
How far it follows a target before giving up. It aggroes at the larger of attack and chase range.
Random wait before its first shot at a new target. Capped at 5 s. Blank uses 1 s.

Rewards & spawning

What a pilot gets for the kill, and where to make the NPC appear.

Before the server's bounty rate. With 0 the kill also costs no faction standing.
Share of the killer's distance to +5.0 a kill gives. Stock rats use 0.1 to 1. Blank uses the server's flat rate (only with a bounty); 0 gives nothing.

Where it spawns

Faction, owner corporation and role (normal, commander, officer) are set where the NPC is placed, not here. The faction decides who it fights alongside and whose standing drops when it dies; the role picks commander and officer loot.
  • Asteroid belts: add it to a region on Region Spawns with a faction and role.
  • Dungeons and sites: place it in a room in the dungeon editor; the dungeon sets the faction.
  • Testing: in space, type /spawn {{if $s.TypeID}}{{$s.TypeID}}{{else}}<typeID>{{end}}. It spawns with no faction, so it fights everyone.
