Squad Tiers

How hard a belt is. Each wave draws one tier, weighted, from those covering the system's security.

Weight is relative within a band, and the % beside it is the real share. Size Δ adjusts the rule's wave size above. Cmdr % and Officer % are the per-wave chances.

Sec floor limits a tier to smaller hulls by filtering ordinary ships at the floor instead of the system's security. 0 means no floor. Commanders and officers ignore it.

⚠ Read at boot. Restart the server for tier and rarity edits to take effect.
