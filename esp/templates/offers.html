Agent Offers

LP-store special offers shown in the client journal "Offers" tab and in agent conversations. An offer trades reward items for a payment of items, ISK and loyalty points. Who presents it is an allow-list per axis — corporation, faction, division — and an offer that narrows none of them is presented by every agent in the game.

Notice sweep

Nothing else pushes an offer at a player — it sits in a journal tab most never open. Every 30 minutes the sweep finds the offers each pilot could already take (the LP they hold with that agent plus the ISK in their wallet, the same two numbers the accept path checks) and rolls once per pilot. On a hit the agent mails them about one of them. One roll and at most one letter per pilot per sweep, and the same offer will not come round again for that pilot until the cooldown is up. Cost items are not checked — an offer taking ore is something to go and mine for. Set the chance to 0 to turn the sweep off.
