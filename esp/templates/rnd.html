Patent Lottery

Each R&D agent runs its own allocation round. Every active project with that agent is an entry and its current RP is the ticket count; one winner takes a Tech II BPO from their own field's pool, collected in person at the agent's station. The whole agent's RP is then wiped and the next round opens — a round is a race, not a savings account.

 · 

     

Synthetic pilots (rolebots) enter the same draw with their RP scaled by the weight above — a roster of a few thousand would otherwise take nearly every patent in the game. At 0% they hold no tickets but are still entrants, and their RP is still wiped: the round ended for the agent, not for the people in it. Changing the round length affects the next round each agent opens, not the ones already running.
