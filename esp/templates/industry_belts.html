Asteroid Belts

What a system's belts hold, and why. Ore is decided by security status first — the empire that owns the space only gates the lowsec specials and the ice. The survey below is produced by running the live belt generator over a scratch copy of the system, so it is the real distribution, not a second guess at it.

Persisted belt fields

A system's belts are stored as one seed, not as rows of rocks — the generator rebuilds the identical field from it on every load, so a hub that empties for ten seconds comes back to the same belt. What is stored per rock is only what has been mined out of it. Regenerating re-rolls the seeds: every system gets a brand new field, and every mined rock is forgotten.

How mining works here

Rocks are sized by cargo volume, not unit count — a 20,000 m³ Arkonor rock and a 20,000 m³ Veldspar rock render the same size and take the same time to strip.
A depleted rock respawns after 5 minutes at its original size, re-rolling only its variant (base/5%/10%) — never its ore.
Ice needs an ice harvester; Mercoxit needs a deep core mining laser; an ice harvester cannot chew ordinary ore.
Mining crystals match a rock by its ore group, so a Veldspar crystal only boosts yield on Veldspar rocks.
