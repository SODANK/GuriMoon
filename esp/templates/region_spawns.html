Commander & Officer Rarity

Global dials over the per-tier chances on Squad Tiers. Those author the texture; these scale it all at once and hold the hard limits. The two % multipliers are relative to what each tier says — 100 means "as authored", 200 doubles every tier's chance.

The chances only decide how often the game asks for an officer. The ledger below decides whether it gets one: at most max alive in a region at a time, and nothing at all in that region for the cool-down after each kill. Wind the chance to its ceiling and the ledger still holds the line.

⚠ These are read once at boot, like the squad tiers. A save here does not affect a running server until it is restarted.
