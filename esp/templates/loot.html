Rebalance Every Drop Table

dropChance is a relative weight, not an independent chance — a kill picks one entry per haul slot with probability weight ÷ pool total. Twenty entries authored at "10%" are 5% each; a "50%" entry beside them takes 21%, not half. Rescaling a whole pool by one factor changes nothing about what drops, it just makes the number on each row mean what it says.

This sweeps every NPC group and type override in the game and puts each role pool on a 100% budget. Commander-only entries are budgeted separately (they get one guaranteed pick on a commander kill). Nothing else changes — no entry is added, removed, or reordered, and every item keeps its exact share of its pool.
