How the numbers work

Units is what you set — the order volume this corp stocks at each of its stations; the group and panel totals are those units summed, still per station. Multiply by {{.Corp.StationCount}} for the whole corp. Price is what reseeding will charge. Left blank it is derived — the item's basePrice for supply, 90% of it for demand, ±5% jitter — and the greyed number in the box is that derived price. Type a figure and it is used exactly, with no jitter and no buy discount; clear the box to go back to auto.

Stored on disk as supplyDemand = units ÷ {{.UnitScale}}, negative for buy. In the original CCP data that value is a normalized share (0–1, usually an exact 1/N fraction) of what the corp trades in a category — this server reads only its sign (supply vs demand) and its magnitude (volume).

Typical values in the shipped data: 833 units (1/12, the most common by far), 909 (1/11), 2,000, 3,333 (1/3), 10,000 (the maximum). Staying in the 100–10,000 range keeps new entries consistent with everything else. A ⚠ row is unpublished or has no market group, so reseeding skips it entirely.
