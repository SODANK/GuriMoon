GuriMoon — GM Slash Command Reference
=====================================

In-game commands typed into any chat channel with a "/" prefix. The reply is
echoed back to the caller only, in the same channel.

Access is gated by the account role bit set at login ("authentication.role" in
config.json). With the shipped autoaccount defaults every account is a GM.

Commands marked [stub] parse and reply but perform no action.


SEARCH & ITEMS
--------------

/search <text or regex>
    Search invTypes by name. Regex accepted. Use this to find the typeID that
    /createitem, /create, /fit and /giveskill take.

/createitem <typeID> [quantity]
    Spawn an item into your station hangar. Quantity defaults to 1.

/create <typeID> [quantity]
    Spawn an item into your ship's cargo hold. This is the command the item
    info window's "GML: Create in cargo" button issues.

/fit <typeID> <slotFlag>
    Fit a module to your current ship. Slot flags: hi 11-18, med 19-26,
    lo 27-34.


CHARACTER
---------

/giveisk <characterID> <amount>
    Add ISK to a character's wallet. Pass 0 for the characterID to target
    yourself.

/giveskill <typeID> <level>
    Grant one skill at level 1-5.

/giveallskill
    Grant yourself every skill at level 5. Aliases: /giveallskills,
    /allskills. Relog to see the change.

/setcorp <corporationID>
    Change your corporation.

/redoportrait <character name or ID>
    Flag a character for portrait recustomization. At the account's next
    character-select the character is hidden and the player re-enters the
    creation flow; only the new appearance is applied to the existing
    character.
/redoportrait clear <character name or ID>
    Unflag.


MOVEMENT
--------

/moveto <stationID>
    Teleport to a station. Updates the database and sends a session change.

/translocate <entityID>
    Teleport to a solar system, station, or any mapDenormalize celestial.
/tr me <entityID>
    Same as /translocate.

/goto <x> <y> <z>
    Snap your ship to a position in the current solar system.

/deadspace
    Teleport to a random isolated spot in the current system.

/selfdestruct
    Destroy your ship or pod, running the full death loop. Requires an
    in-flight session.


GM MODE
-------

/gm on
/gm off
    While on, nothing in the world — player, drone or NPC — may lock, approach
    or shoot you, and NPCs neither notice you nor take threat from you.

    This is a TARGETING RULE, NOT INVULNERABILITY. A scripted dungeon effect
    or /selfdestruct still applies. Turning it on also sweeps you out of every
    threat table on the node, because the target pickers only refuse the next
    lock.

    The flag lives in memory, not in a column: a server restart clears it.

/gm cloak on [solid]
/gm cloak off
    A standing cloak for filming. Unlike the gate-arrival cloak it is never
    broken by movement, warp, a gate or a module, so prop mods run under it;
    it survives docking, gates and a pod transition. You are hidden from every
    other pilot and untargetable (it turns /gm on as well), and you can still
    lock targets yourself.

    Unless you say "solid", your own ball is put on the wire as 1 m wide, so
    your client has no ship to overlap-resolve against (collision lives in the
    client's destiny.dll, not here). That radius is a client-side guess: it may
    also shrink your ship model or move the camera. If it does, use "solid".

    Off leaves /gm on alone; use /gm off to be targetable again. In memory
    only, like /gm on.


DIAGNOSTICS
-----------

/scene, /stamps and /ghosts are measurements, not powers; /refresh acts on them. Read them against what the client is
drawing.

/scene [filter]
    Print the SERVER's distance to every ball on your grid, one line per ball.
    Read it beside the client's own overview distance column: the difference on
    any row is client-vs-server drift, measured live instead of inferred. A
    ball reading 8 km on the overview and 22 km here is a ball whose wreck and
    explosion will land 14 km from where the pilot watched the ship die.

/stamps
    Report your destiny stamp stream: mints per second against the
    one-per-second budget, how far the stream has run ahead of the wall clock,
    and which server call sites spent the stamps. The companion to /scene —
    /scene shows that a ball is in the wrong place, this shows why.

/ghosts
    Explain, ball by ball, why your overview looks the way it does: which rule
    is keeping each ball, or that the server has forgotten it entirely (in
    which case no prune will ever offer to remove it and the row is permanent).

/refresh [hard]
    Dump stale overview entries. Plain: remove every ball the server knows is
    stale and reload the overview from your own ballpark, which also clears a
    ship listed twice (delivered twice, removed once). Hard: rebuild the whole
    ballpark first (SetState), which is the only cure for a ball the server
    has forgotten. The plain reload also runs by itself ~4 s after every
    undock, gate jump and ballpark resync.


SOVEREIGNTY & STARBASES
-----------------------

/claim [systemID]
    Claim 0.0 sovereignty for your alliance. Defaults to the current system.

/unclaim [systemID]
    Release sovereignty held by your alliance.

/sov [systemID|list]
    Show sovereignty for the current system, a named system, or — with "list" —
    all of your alliance's holdings.

/posplant <typeID> <moonID>
    Anchor a control tower at a moon. Requires alliance sovereignty.

/posonline <entityID>
    Bring an anchored starbase online.

/posremove <entityID>
    Remove a starbase (admin teardown).

/poslist [systemID]
    List starbases in the current or a named system.

/fuel <days> <entityID>
    Load N days of fuel into a starbase's fuel bay.

/possave <towerID> <name> [fuelDays]
    Capture a player-built POS as a template: the tower type, every anchored
    structure's type and offset from the tower, what each handles, and the
    Production wiring. Replaces a template of the same name.

/posspawn <name> <moonID> [online] [fuelDays]
    Stamp a template at a moon, owned by your corporation. Placement rules are
    /posplant's (security ceiling, one tower per moon, sovereignty). "online"
    brings the tower and the template's online structures up at once;
    fuelDays overrides the template's fuel.

/postemplates
    List POS templates.

/moonscan [moonID]
    Survey a moon into the Moon Analysis tab. Defaults to the nearest moon.
    Pushes OnMoonScanComplete — the same event a survey probe will deliver once
    the probe-launch path exists.


MISSIONS
--------

/mission list
    List mission templates (first 30).
/mission give <templateID>
    Offer a template to yourself from an agent at your current station.
/mission info
    Show your current mission.
/mission complete
    Force-complete your current mission.
/mission clear
    Clear your current mission.


MARKET
------

/seedmarkets
    Seed every published item at your current station. Wipes existing Seeder
    orders at that station.

/seednpcmarkets
    Regenerate all NPC corporation market orders globally, from
    crpnpccorporationtrades.


SPACE SIMULATION
----------------

/gateflash
    Fire the gate animation on nearby stargates.

/concord
    Trigger a CONCORD response against yourself (test harness).

/police [navy]
    Send the system's police (or navy) squad against yourself now, whatever
    your record (test harness). It stands down after the linger if your record
    does not qualify.

/secstatus <value>
    Set your own security status (-10 to 10) through the ordinary write path.

/customs
    Trigger a customs response against yourself (test harness).
